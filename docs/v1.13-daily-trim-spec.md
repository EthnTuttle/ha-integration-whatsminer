# v1.13 daily_trim: the daily plan as the PID's envelope

Status: implemented behind the **Control strategy** option (default `pid`, so the update changes nothing until it is switched on). Code: `controller.py` (the band intersection in `_run_pid_step`, `DAILY_TRIM_BAND`, `_publish_daily`), `const.py` (the strategy value), `sensor.py` (the `daily_trim_active` attribute). Tests: `tests/test_daily_trim.py`.

---

## 1. Motivation

`daily` (1.10.0) sends one power a day and holds it, betting the plan is good enough. On a wrong plan the supply parks off target for hours — the plan only trims by the 24 h supply mean, computed yesterday. `pid` adapts every tick but pays a btminer restart per move. `daily_trim` is the middle: the plan gives today's operating point, the PID trims around it inside a fixed ±500 W band, using the restarts it already makes, and every safety layer keeps full authority.

## 2. Semantics

`daily_trim` behaves **exactly** like `pid` — soft start, predictive back-off, setpoint ramp, actuation gates, all caps/clamps/lockouts — with one change to `_run_pid_step`: after the floor and the price/surplus envelope set `out_min`/`out_max`, and before the vendored PID sees them, the bounds are intersected with `[P_day − 500, P_day + 500]`:

```
lo = max(out_min, P_day − 500); hi = min(out_max, P_day + 500)
apply only if lo ≤ hi
```

- The band only **narrows** what the PID may command. Every cap/lockout floor enforcement wins exactly as under `pid` — the supply/chip soft cap clamp, the 140°F lockout, the demand lockout/dwell floors, freeze-guard holds and floor enforcement all act downstream of the envelope.
- **Empty interval.** If existing bounds (e.g. a price/surplus envelope far below the band) leave no overlap — `lo > hi` — the band is skipped for that tick and the tick is precisely what `pid` would do.
- **No valid plan yet.** With under 12 h of samples the plan holds the current limit (power_w is `None`) and retries within the hour, as under `daily`. Until a plan lands, every tick is exactly `pid`. The plan's own retry cadence is unchanged.
- **Plan updates.** A recomputed plan applies on the next tick — the envelope moves with it (subject to the PID's usual actuation gates for actually sending a command).
- The daily-plan attributes (`daily_plan_power`, `daily_plan_reason`, …) publish exactly as under `daily`/`daily_observe`.

## 3. The 750 W/day plan rate limit is gone

The 1.10.0 plan computation moved `P_day` at most 750 W from the previous plan. That smoothing was written for open-loop `daily`, where a bad plan holds all day. With `daily_trim` absorbing the plan's error live, the lag is a liability. The step is removed for both strategies; the 50 W rounding and the floor..Power Max clamp stay.

## 4. Config and observability

- Configure → first step → **Control strategy** gains `daily_trim`. **Daily plan hour** (!default 4) as before.
- The Control Mode sensor gains `daily_trim_active` (boolean): true only while the strategy is `daily_trim` and a valid plan is in force.
- The existing `out_min_effective`/`out_max_effective` diagnostics show the narrowed envelope (they are computed from the same `out_min`/`out_max` the band modifies).

## 5. Honest limits

- The plan still needs ≥12 h of power samples; a fresh install is plain `pid` until then.
- The band is ±500 W. A plan off by more than that caps the loop a flat 500 W away — the supply will sit off target until the next plan hour.
- The all-idle demand lockout and dwell floors still clamp to the floor inside the band; nothing in `daily_trim` raises output above what `pid` would command.
